# M4 coastline — M2-informed provisional resource envelope

Date: 2026-10-20. The user accepted the numbers below **as provisional measurement gates only**, not as an operating budget, target-cost result, asset/renderer approval or firmware authorization. This draws from the [frozen S3 M2 results](s3-m2.md), [V2 kernel scaling](s3-expanded.md), [design's 20 Hz intent](../DESIGN.md#hardware) and [offline coastline work proxy](m4-coastline-fallback-appearance-study.md). The pinned M2 evidence is not updated or rerun; there is no hardware access.

## Relevant measured facts — not spare capacity

- The display intent is **400×240, 1-bit, 20 Hz**, a nominal **50 ms frame period**. Sharp transfer/refresh, full app/render, networking, concurrent pass prediction and actual frame latency are **not measured** on the S3. A stored 352×240 wide-radar 1-bit layer alone is **10,560 bytes** (84,480 bits); a full 400×240 image is **12,000 bytes**. Neither buffer is granted by existing M2 measurements.
- The V2 240 MHz S3 benchmark reports **4.8295 ms per four-slot tracking tick**, **19.1947 ms per 16-slot tick** and **76.6862 ms per 64-slot tick**, each an *amortized mean across 1,441 ticks*, not an individual maximum. The 64-slot grid already exceeds 50 ms **before rendering**. Catalogue composition, age, calculation cadence and the actual app are different. Synchronous search is seconds to tens of seconds; frozen M2 case 06 aggregation median is **25.567 s**. Neither can simply share a 50 ms display loop.
- The frozen catalogue benchmark used a **65,536-byte reserved internal heap** and observed maximum pipeline occupancy **32,124 B** in its isolated cases. That is **not 33,412 B of proven spare RAM**: the fixed heap is already in `.bss`, stack-safe size is not proved, and there is no display/network/application concurrency. Observed written-stack depth peaks at **9,248 B** in initialization, including probe/paint overhead; it is not a worst-case stack allowance.
- The provisional Natural Earth 40 km / 1e-4° trial encoding is **15,484 bytes**, not an adopted asset. Across four offline sites × five FOVs, the provisional local-gap path reaches **15,806 geographic front steps** and **17,857 stroke pixel-distance candidates** in one polar 20° scene. These are Python work counts, not S3 cycles. The [Q52 checked fixed-point control](m4-coastline-fixed-interval-feasibility.md) leaves near-edge culls unknown and Q56 overflows in the tested algebra; runtime trig and arbitrary geography are not implemented.

## Accepted provisional *measurement targets*, not a claim of availability

| Coastline-specific provisional gate | Proposed ceiling | Rationale / explicit exclusion |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| **Full coast rebuild including visibility, sampling, clipping, uncertain/fallback decision and ink** | **≤5 ms maximum observed invocation** at 240 MHz across a specified observer/zoom and adversarial-crossing suite | At most **10% of nominal 50 ms** on the tested suite; a review target, **not** derived free time. Whole-device app/Sharp/scheduler must still prove enough headroom; limited samples cannot establish a hard worst-case bound. If it cannot fit, do not silently shorten the coastline or block tracking. |
| **Incremental compiled image contribution** (coast data **plus** executable code/tables) | **≤64 KiB**; within it **≤32 KiB** for a checked exported coastline asset | A deliberately conservative *review cap* relative to the unapproved 15,484-byte trial, not a partition/flash-capacity calculation. Existing benchmark image bytes and an 8 MB device do not specify usable app space. Reject an export/build over cap; do not silently truncate coast. Existing image gate remains required. |
| **Incremental dynamic heap** | **0 B** | Keep platform-agnostic coast path no_std and predictable; do not infer the isolated 64 KiB heap's unused bytes are available. |
| **Incremental live internal scratch/stack attributable to coast** | **≤1 KiB measured increment**; **no persistent coast-only bitmap** by default | A proposed measurement threshold, **not a safe stack bound**. Full device task stack, interrupts and omitted firmware are unknown. Pixel buffer/caching would require separately budgeting ≥10,560 B for wide or 12,000 B full-frame (before metadata/stride), changing this proposal. |
| **Work exhaustion and numeric unknowns** | **No coast drawn from uncertified fragments; no partial frame quietly accepted** | Operation/segment limits and whole-geography fallback threshold remain **unselected**. A failure must leave satellite/limb/notice truth unchanged, not masquerade as empty sky. |

At 240 MHz a 5 ms coast slice is only **1.2 million cycles**. The measured host proxy's 15,806 steps + 17,857 pixel-distance candidates are **33,663 units before** sign/cull, trig, clipping, loop overhead and drawing. A naive equal division gives ~**36 cycles per unit**—*not* a measured cost, a cycle estimate for any one operation, or a proof of infeasibility. Double precision and checked i128 may be expensive on this target; neither was timed in a coastline build.

**Conflict to resolve before a runtime contract:** “suppress the whole geography layer after any over-budget/uncertain failure” cannot be made atomic by drawing directly into the shared 1-bit target and only then discovering failure. A reversible layer requires extra memory; a preflight/two-pass path requires additional time and must include both passes within the same gate; a deterministic up-front reject rule would need its own proof. No such architecture is selected here. The earlier coast-only fallback poster contains no foreground and does not solve this compositing constraint. No 20 Hz support, 5 ms coastline viability, safe 1 KiB stack increment, 64 KiB flash availability or acceptable cap/fallback appearance is demonstrated.

## Review gate

These ceilings are accepted **only to screen and measure candidate designs**. Separately establish M2 supported input/refresh cadence and **whole-device** frame headroom, including Sharp output, before turning any ceiling into an operating guarantee. A target image could then check compiled sections/data and maximum observed coastline time/scratch at the pinned prospective coast source plus adversarial unknown/viewport cases; do not use the historical frozen M2 image as the coast build. Any flash or toolchain change needs explicit permission. This proposal does **not** authorize firmware, flashing, an asset export, coast renderer or a new M2 capture matrix.
