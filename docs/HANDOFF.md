## Work retained
A checked [offline export](../render/assets/README.md) and static 15,484-byte asset now drive a first no-heap wide-only surface-coast comparison in `render/src/coast.rs`, using shared `app/src/projection.rs` camera semantics. It interpolates, splits front-facing runs, clips and draws 1-bit strokes beneath the foreground, and repaints on coast-only failure with `COAST ERROR`. A draw-target error fails the frame. Host regressions exercise SF zooms, asset corruption, clipping and square/circular isolation; [fresh executable review frames](mockups/0061-first-executable-coastline-review.md) retain SF 0.1°/1°/5°/100°, Colorado 20°, polar 20° and dateline 5°. The 40 km export is an **authorized comparison asset**, not production approval; no new dependency, firmware, hardware or toolchain change.

**Limitations:** fixed 0.2° endpoint/midpoint sampling and integer line raster can miss a narrower screen-relevant excursion or produce changed pixels at tangency. Finite work and a checked decoded asset are not proof that [0053](decisions/0053-no-resolvable-coastline-omissions.md) is satisfied on every ordinary scene. No coast-specific S3 time/stack, Sharp throughput, compiled-size delta or whole-device 20 Hz capacity has been measured. [Provisional measurement gates](evaluations/m4-coastline-budget-preflight.md) remain ≤5 ms observed rebuild, ≤64 KiB compiled addition (≤32 KiB asset), zero heap, ≤1 KiB measured incremental scratch/stack—none proven spare. Do not refresh the [frozen M2 evidence](decisions/0023-frozen-m2-revision-isolation.md).
