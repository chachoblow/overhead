Board now runs the **device app (`overhead-s3-device`, banner `OVERHEAD_DEVICE_V2`, `visual` group, knob zoom) with 0075's 8-hour refresh at the user's home site** (local build from the ignored `firmware/.wifi.env` and `firmware/.site.env`; the site is personal data, never commit/print it). Board access, reset, capture and flash no longer need approval ([0069](decisions/0069-flash-without-per-flash-approval.md)); still confirm the port and gate the image. Do not change toolchains without asking.
- Development moved to devbox (Linux x86_64) on 2026-10-31. The board will be plugged in there (`/dev/ttyUSB*` or `/dev/ttyACM*`): confirm the port and MAC before flashing. Devbox flashing was verified on 2026-11-01: the board is on `/dev/ttyUSB0` (CP2102N, MAC confirmed); [evidence](../firmware/README.md#historical-multi-marker-boot-cycle-diagnostic-flashed-short-uart-capture).

## BOOT (no step queued)
- [0068](decisions/0068-boot-selection-gesture.md): in-view-only ascending cycling via 0049, one event per completed debounced press with no hold-repeat, and presses without a valid frame discarded. `firmware/src/button_input.rs` is host-tested and composed with `RadarApp::select_next()`; active-low GPIO0/pull-up and 20 ms are **provisional diagnostic choices**, not production timing. Knob zoom stays separate.
- [0070](decisions/0070-boot-input-capture-independent-of-render.md) interrupt capture (`firmware/src/boot_capture.rs`, 5 ms timer) is used by `sharp-boot-capture` (TIMG0) and `live-utc-boot` (TIMG1); see the [BOOT sampling-gap review](evaluations/boot-sampling-gaps.md). Both user tap sessions passed on 2026-11-01: 23 presses on the historical image, 44 on live UTC (`applied=1 valid=true`, largest gaps ~5–6.5 ms, once 9.2 ms). **Visible live-UTC cycling** was then user-observed on `live-fetch-radar` (five CSS-complex objects in view, ten presses, two full cycles). [Details](../firmware/README.md#opt-in-boot-selection-on-the-live-utc-diagnostic-interrupt-capture-user-tap-session).
- Visible cycling was confirmed earlier on the fixed-historical [`sharp-boot-cycle` image](../firmware/README.md#historical-multi-marker-boot-cycle-diagnostic-flashed-short-uart-capture) (`19548 → 21639 → 22314`, user saw each change); that is bounded physical evidence, **not live UTC** or guaranteed short-press/hold/timing behavior. Raw captures and credential-bearing artifacts are ignored local files; never commit/share them.
- Open BOOT risks: a ~34–41 ms sampling gap during Wi-Fi association at every boot (reconnects unmeasured); over 10.5 h about 2% of 10 s windows had 10–16 ms gaps away from fetches (under the 20 ms debounce); multi-press drains (host-tested only), press latency and duration.
