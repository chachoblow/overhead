Devbox builds are therefore **not byte-identical** to recorded Mac ELF/image hashes: a rebuilt frozen M2 pair differed but passed the image gate. Treat devbox images as new builds, never as the recorded ones. Devbox flashing was verified on 2026-11-01: the board is on `/dev/ttyUSB0` (CP2102N, MAC confirmed); [evidence](../firmware/README.md#historical-multi-marker-boot-cycle-diagnostic-flashed-short-uart-capture).
