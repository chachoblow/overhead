# M4 — Coastline source, geometry and raster contract for review

**Proposal, not an accepted geometry/raster contract or implementation authorization.** The user agreed to draft a contract for review and separately accepted [0052's conditional Natural Earth source candidate](../decisions/0052-natural-earth-coastline-candidate.md), not to bundle an asset or change the renderer. [0051](../decisions/0051-angular-coastline-appearance-direction.md) accepts only the angular 1-bit *appearance* at 5°–100°. The [local](0052-local-coastline-comparison.md), [intermediate](0053-coastline-zoom-progression.md) and [overview](0054-coastline-overview-comparison.md) images are offline OR composites, not executable output or proof of clipping, layering, cost or panel legibility. This proposal supersedes neither those records nor the [earlier rules sketch](0051-coastline-rules-proposal.md).

## Proposed boundary

- Coastlines are static **surface geography** in the opt-in 352×240 wide radar only. The square/circular diagnostic baseline stays byte-for-byte unchanged. Use the existing fixed 75,000 km camera, north-up lens, WGS-84 zero-height ellipsoid and unfilled projected limb; do not move the aim point or stretch the globe. No coast on an invalid Earth view.
- Show angular 1px black strokes on white throughout the reviewed 5°–100° range, including the tiny overview cue. The user also agreed to **pursue the same angular treatment at 0.1°–<5° subject to visual and work-budget evidence**, rather than assuming a cutoff. Using the same data without a new detailed dataset is a *proposal*, not an accepted source/detail rule. At those tighter zooms some views may contain no coastline; blank geography is not a tracking or ingestion notice. Compare 0.1°, 1° and adjacent zooms at coastal and inland sites before settling visibility or raster/detail rules. Do not turn the 5° preference into an unsupported promise of city/Bay detail.
- Keep true observer elevation > 0 as the satellite eligibility rule, the existing near plane and viewport rules for markers/arrows, the limb, notices, selected-ID continuity and five-field strip. Coastline visibility is **camera-facing surface visibility**, not an additional satellite-occultation rule. Do not fill land, erase a marker behind Earth, alter counts, select by geography, or change physical state.

## Candidate input and bounded preparation (source candidate agreed; export unapproved)

- [0052](../decisions/0052-natural-earth-coastline-candidate.md) accepts the same [Natural Earth 1:110m v4.1.0 WGS-84 coastline archive, SHA-256 and public-domain provenance](0051-coastline-legibility-comparison.md) as a **conditional candidate** for the first bounded slice. Its 134 polylines / 5,128 source vertices and `scalerank` field are *source facts*, not a packed size or target-work measurement. Reverify source terms/version/hash before exporting; no runtime fetching or new crate by default. Keep a fixture provenance/regeneration note if data is later committed.
- Offline generation should validate finite coordinates, closed/open source parts, explicit antimeridian handling and polyline boundaries. Encode bounded, read-only geographic coordinates with part breaks in a platform-neutral asset; no shapefile/JSON parsing or heap allocation per frame. Preserve enough intermediate surface samples to avoid chords crossing an invisible hemisphere or skipping a visible bend. Do **not** adopt the preview's `scalerank == 0`, projected 2.5px RDP or 0.2° interpolation as an export rule merely because the composites looked angular. Test any proposed simplification on multiple observers and zooms, including coast near the viewport edge and tangent. No joins across part, antimeridian, hidden or clipped gaps.
- The [initial offline size/work preflight](../evaluations/m4-coastline-source-preflight.md) and [40/75 km reduction/clip study](../evaluations/m4-coastline-reduction-study.md) measure trial bytes, sampled scan work and [native 1-bit composites](0056-coastline-reduction-comparison.md). The user chose **40 km for further offline study only**. Its [decoded-coordinate/culling diagnostic](../evaluations/m4-coastline-40km-refinement.md) and [0.2°/0.05°/0.01° sampling comparison](../evaluations/m4-coastline-sampling-study.md) show that finer geographic steps alter 1-bit raster ink without settling a pixel-stable algorithm, a target-float/curve error bound, executable layering or a budget. A proposed export must still publish reproducible retained parts/vertices/segments, actual encoded bytes, maximum segment angular span and worst-case projection/subdivision/clipping/raster operations across sites/FOVs. Specify a checked maximum asset size and work bound after that design is measured; reject an over-limit export rather than silently truncating geography at runtime. Frozen M2 kernel captures do not include coastline rendering; host observations are not target RAM or safe-stack evidence. Production capacity/cadence needs separate M2 work.

## Proposed geometry and compositing semantics

1. Convert consecutive points of each validated surface part to WGS-84 zero-height ECEF, using the shared camera transform for perspective projection. Determine front-facing surface by the outward ellipsoid normal dotted with the vector from the surface point **toward the camera**, not observer elevation and not a 2D limb-circle test. Treat exact tangency as a boundary, never as evidence that the far side is visible. Reject nonfinite and near-plane-invalid projections; no reversed, unbounded pixel coordinates.
2. Split each geographic curve into bounded surface intervals before clipping. For an interval crossing front/back, locate the tangent crossing on that surface curve with a finite, deterministic iteration limit; retain only the front-facing portion. An interval with back-facing endpoints must not be joined through the far side; preparation/subdivision must account for an interior visible run. Then project the surviving portion and clip **segments**, not only endpoints, against the wide drawable rectangle before converting to pixel integers. Crossing the viewport with both endpoints off-screen should still draw the in-view portion. Do not connect across a clipped-out or hidden gap. Any approximation tolerance, maximum subdivision and failure behavior must be documented with measured error at the 0.1° end before this becomes an implementation contract.
3. Draw coastline and the existing limb as background linework, then aim point/N, arrows, markers, selected ID, strip and finally 0040/0048's white-backed notices. Strokes are on/off only, without fill, shading, dithering or a mask. Existing foreground ink must remain visible even when a coastline runs through it; black-on-black overlaps may visually merge. Specify/inspect the exact coast–limb and coast–aim-point raster junctions on fresh executable frames before accepting this order. The earlier overlays OR black ink over *finished* frames, so they do not validate it.
4. Always clear/repaint from the current frame as today: zooming or changing observer must leave no old coastline ink. Geography cannot supply a selected object, a notice or a false claim about the catalogue. Keep no per-frame geography history.

## Evidence and decisions before implementation authorization

- The named source/version is agreed as a *candidate*, not an exported asset. Review which **offline** reduction/encoding preserves 0051's angular look without obvious popping from 0.1° to 100°. The direction to explore angular coastlines below 5° is agreed; exact tight-zoom detail/visibility and evidence remain open, not settled by the paragraph above.
- Use the [source preflight](../evaluations/m4-coastline-source-preflight.md), [reduction study](../evaluations/m4-coastline-reduction-study.md), [40 km diagnostic](../evaluations/m4-coastline-40km-refinement.md) and [sampling comparison](../evaluations/m4-coastline-sampling-study.md) to refine a **specific export and renderer proposal** with pixel-stable raster treatment, bounded *sample and target-float* screen error, checked maxima and explicit over-budget behavior. The offline coarse-cull trial is not a target-cost measurement. Review compiled asset/target storage and renderer work against an explicit budget before treating it as device-ready. Do not refresh or reinterpret [frozen M2 evidence](../evaluations/s3-m2/README.md).
- Once independently authorized, implement only in platform-neutral app/render code plus an offline preparation tool/asset if approved. Test convex ellipsoid front-face and tangent crossings at both poles, dateline, high-latitude and edge-on locations; two-off-screen-endpoint viewport crossings; finite near-plane/zoom extremes; open-part and hidden-gap isolation; deterministic 1-bit pixels; full-frame clearing; selected/empty/notice overlaps; unchanged square/circular bytes, markers, arrows, counts, selection and true-state fields. Compare fresh executable wide snapshots with native-size no-coast controls at Colorado and San Francisco, selected and empty, from 0.1° through 100°. Run workspace check/tests and app/render no-default-feature checks. User review of **executable** output is separate from contract approval; Sharp-panel legibility and S3 performance still require separate validation.

**Open for review:** whether a bounded export from the agreed source candidate can be adopted, its simplification/format and numerical bounds, tangent/viewport approximation tolerances, below-5° detail/visibility evidence, and pixel junctions. Until those are agreed, this document is a testable proposal, not a numbered decision or permission to implement.
