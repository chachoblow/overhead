# M4 — First executable coastline slice: decision proposal

**For review, not an accepted contract or implementation authorization.** The user wants coastlines and asked to move from open-ended offline studies toward one limited, buildable slice. This narrows the older [source/geometry proposal](0055-coastline-contract-proposal.md); it does not amend [0051's appearance direction](../decisions/0051-angular-coastline-appearance-direction.md), adopt [0052's conditional source](../decisions/0052-natural-earth-coastline-candidate.md), select a raster/encoding or promise target capacity. No implementation, export, firmware, hardware work or further offline study is authorized by this document.

## Product boundary to review

- First executable comparison: **opt-in 352×240 wide radar only**, static angular 1-bit coastline on the existing WGS-84 Earth surface. Keep the camera, unfilled limb, satellite horizon eligibility, markers/arrows, aim point, notices, selection, glyph strip, square/circular modes and physical state unchanged. Draw coast as *background* beneath foreground/white-backed notices, without land fill or a second coastline bitmap.
- Preserve the reviewed **5°–100°** appearance. Include **0.1°–<5° in executable visual tests**, but do not promise detail or a coastline in every local scene: 0051 agreed to investigate that range, not its appearance. No arbitrary tight-zoom switch-off, change of source scale, or invented detail is implicit. If the candidate fails tight-zoom review, bring the result back for a separate scope decision rather than silently disabling it.
- Coastline is visual context, never tracking truth. **A resolvable visible coastline must not acquire a local gap or disappear because the renderer reached an illustrative uncertainty or work cutoff.** If the chosen algorithm cannot resolve ordinary valid scenes within its finite limits, fix or replace the algorithm before shipping; do not treat degraded geography as an accepted result. Genuinely invalid input or an unexpected runtime failure still needs a safe whole-frame/coast-failure policy that preserves Earth/marker/notice truth; its presentation is **not yet agreed**. Do not label such a frame `NONE ABOVE HORIZON` on geography's behalf or conflate the failure with ingestion.

## One finite candidate path, not a selected algorithm

1. Prepare a **versioned, checked, read-only asset** offline from the pinned Natural Earth candidate. Validate coordinates, part boundaries/antimeridian, maximum segment span, count and encoded bytes. Define and reject breaches of fixed compile-time bounds; never quietly truncate the source. The 40 km reduction, 1e-4° encoding and 0.2° geographic stepping are study inputs, **not defaults**. No runtime shapefile parsing, fetching, heap or additional dependency without explaining why.
2. Use a **fixed-capacity, per-segment pending stack** and finite subdivision/node counters to classify camera-facing surface runs. Front/back, tangent, near-plane, viewport and numeric uncertainty have separate, explicit outcomes; an undecidable interval is **unknown, not invisible**. Resolve relevant ordinary unknowns by a justified, finite refinement or alternate method; a cap that regularly creates missing coast is not acceptable. Clip visible projected segments before integer raster; never connect across a source break or hidden interval. Use finite checked arithmetic and conservative error treatment for the selected *bounded* asset/observer/FOV domain. A broad theorem about every conceivable coastline curve is not required, but host-f64 estimates alone are **not** target-arithmetic bounds.
3. Put a **finite whole-geography work limit** around the operation, not only a per-segment node cap. Bound and test the supported input/observer/FOV domain so valid supported scenes finish within it; a budget-exhausted but otherwise resolvable scene is **a failed design gate**, not a normal coast-dropout mode. A *preflight* must account for every source segment, projected step and candidate raster operation before ink is written. If it commits, inputs are immutable for the frame and the pixel path must have checked, finite bounds with no additional coast-specific failure that preflight could not have ruled out. If that cannot be established, do **not** stream ink into an irrevocable shared frame: propose and cost a recovery/repaint strategy or reject two-pass commit. A physical draw/transfer failure remains a whole-frame failure, not a coast-only success.
4. **Do not adopt the studied local-gap/whole-geography fallback as normal presentation.** `0.001°/8`, a 16-entry pending stack and other trial caps are illustrative only, not acceptable visual-omission thresholds. Certify that genuinely non-visible/zero-ink uncertainty may be omitted *only if its lack of screen effect is justified*; mere small angular span is insufficient near tight zoom or tangency. For a visible fragment, refine/resolve it or reject the candidate method at review. Specify separately what happens on corrupted input or unexpected failure; do not quietly treat those as ordinary blank-coast frames.

## Exit criteria: build or stop, not another series of studies

| Gate | Pass evidence for a proposed implementation | If it fails |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contract review | Agreement on the supported input domain/format, treatment that resolves screen-relevant uncertainty, finite per-segment **and global** limits covering supported scenes, raster, no-late-commit-failure strategy and explicit exceptional-failure handling. | Fix/simplify the implementation, negotiate a *separate* supported scope, or reject this architecture. Do not silently accept missing resolvable geography or continue open-ended predicate probes. |
| Executable host slice (requires separate authorization) | Fresh actual wide-renderer snapshots at 0.1°, 1°, 5°, 20°, 100° across coastal/inland, horizon, viewport, polar and dateline cases, selected/empty/notice overlaps; byte-identical square/circular baselines; deterministic invalid/unknown/forced-limit tests proving no partial or silently missing resolvable coast. Compare no-coast controls and review native 1-bit pixels. | Revise the concrete artifact/contract or narrow the promised zoom/detail range **with user approval**. Mocks and offline masks alone cannot pass. |
| Target feasibility (requires separate hardware/firmware authorization) | Measure full coast rebuild (both passes and ink), incremental compiled code+asset, heap and measured stack/scratch against [provisional 5 ms / 64 KiB (≤32 KiB asset) / zero-heap / 1 KiB gates](../evaluations/m4-coastline-budget-preflight.md), including an adversarial suite. Separately establish whole-device frame/Sharp-output, stack and image headroom at a supported workload. | Reduce work/detail or change the architecture and request review; do not infer spare capacity from frozen M2 kernels, hide overruns or declare 20 Hz supported. |
| Acceptance | User reviews **fresh executable** coast output; separate physical-panel legibility and whole-device verification before shipping. | No acceptance from an offline composite or passing host tests alone. |

The [two-pass](../evaluations/m4-coastline-atomic-fallback-study.md) and [bounded-streaming](../evaluations/m4-coastline-streaming-enclosure-study.md) trials remain useful *diagnostics of failure*, **not** an accepted strategy to leave local gaps or drop geography for resolvable valid scenes. A trial without a credible answer to screen-relevant unknowns, preflight/commit and finite-work gates should stop *before* bundling an asset or changing the renderer.

**Settled review feedback:** [0053](../decisions/0053-no-resolvable-coastline-omissions.md) rejects local gaps and whole-coast dropouts **when the coastline is resolvable**. Illustrative unknown thresholds cannot become ordinary rendering policy. **Still open:** what exceptional behavior should apply to genuinely invalid asset data or a failure that cannot be resolved at runtime; do not assume a silent blank frame or introduce a new notice without asking. Exact representation/tolerances and implementation/hardware permission also remain open.
